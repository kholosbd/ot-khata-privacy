Privacy Policy for OT Khata - Overtime Calculator

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

Welcome to OT Khata - Overtime Calculator ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information.

2. Information Collection and Storage

OT Khata is designed to be a lightweight and offline-friendly utility app:

  • Local Storage: All data relating to your overtime hours, hourly wages, custom rates, calculations, and notes are stored strictly locally on your own physical mobile device. We do not transmit or store this data on external cloud servers.
  • No Account Needed: You do not need to create an account, register, or provide any sensitive personal credentials to use the application.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To keep the app free and support ongoing maintenance, the app may display advertisements via Google AdMob. These third-party services may collect standard anonymous device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID, IP address, and app performance logs) to deliver relevant ads and detect fraud according to Google Play Policies.

4. Data Security

Because your calculation logs remain directly on your mobile device, your data is as secure as your personal device. We do not sell, rent, or trade any personal data to outside parties.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us immediately.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be posted directly on this web page.
